Today, we’re excited to share that Dropbox plans to acquire Tigris.

<!-- truncate -->

For our customers, the most important thing to know is that nothing about your
experience changes. You’ll keep the same product, the same S3-compatible API,
the same pricing with zero egress fees, and the same team and support channels
you work with today. Tigris continues to run alongside whichever clouds and
compute platforms you choose.

We started Tigris to give developers a storage layer that works the way they
build: one S3-compatible endpoint for their application data, wherever their
compute runs. AI has only made that idea more important. Compute keeps moving
across clouds and providers, and the storage layer is what stays constant. Our
years running storage infrastructure at scale helped us build a product
customers could depend on, and those customers have shaped it ever since: AI
builders generating images and video and working with model artifacts, and the
platforms other developers build and run their software on.

Dropbox has operated storage at massive scale for nearly two decades, and shares
our conviction that applications need a consistent home for their data as AI
reshapes how they are built. Together, we would scale Tigris to meet the demand
our customers keep bringing us and deliver powerful new capabilities.

## Why Dropbox

With Dropbox’s resources and expertise, we’d be better positioned to plan for
growing capacity needs and invest in the service as customer demand evolves. We
also want to keep the direct technical collaboration and developer focus that
help us understand what to build next.

“The value of AI depends on the content and context it can work with,” said
Ashraf Alkarmi, co-CEO of Dropbox. “We’re bringing AI to the work people already
keep in Dropbox and extending our storage expertise to companies building AI
applications. Across both, our goal is to give customers the freedom to choose
how they use AI while keeping control of the content they depend on.”

“AI is driving storage demand at a scale this industry has not seen before.
Meeting it takes massive scale and a conviction we share with Dropbox: that
customers should have the flexibility to run their compute wherever it serves
them best, with a product built for developers and agents alike,” said Ovais
Tariq, co-founder and CEO of Tigris. “Dropbox brings nearly two decades of
operating storage at that scale. We bring the developer-focused product our
customers depend on. Together we would meet that demand across the industry, and
deliver the capabilities that come next, starting with filesystem and content
processing.”

## Object storage first

The planned acquisition would accelerate Dropbox’s infrastructure strategy
through DBX Agent Cloud. That strategy starts with standalone object storage
that companies and developers can adopt independently.

We want to be clear about what this means for the platforms our customers build
on. Tigris is a storage layer, and it will remain one. We built it to work
alongside whichever compute environment a developer chooses, from the platforms
that run their applications to the providers that supply their GPUs, and that
approach continues under Dropbox. The companies that provide those platforms
have been partners to Tigris, and we see this as an opportunity to do more with
them.

Dropbox plans to add filesystem and content-processing capabilities next. Those
capabilities would extend the same storage layer: a filesystem interface onto
the data in Tigris, and processing that turns documents, images, audio, and
video into AI-ready inputs. We’d love to hear how they could help your team, and
we’ll share details as the plans develop.

The broader direction is to give developers a portable foundation for content
across models and compute environments, with the flexibility to choose the tools
that fit their applications.

## What happens next

You can keep using Tigris as you do today, with the same products and services
and your usual contacts for support. We’ll keep you informed as the transaction
progresses. In the meantime, tell us what you’re building and where you’d like
more help. Those conversations will help shape our next steps.

Thank you for the trust you place in Tigris and for helping shape the product.
We’re excited about the opportunity ahead and look forward to continuing to
build with you.

## About Tigris

Tigris is a globally distributed, S3-compatible object storage platform
purpose-built for AI. Founded by former Uber engineers, Tigris enables
developers and enterprises to store and access data across clouds without vendor
lock-in or egress fees. Its intelligent data placement technology delivers
low-latency access worldwide, eliminating the complexity of managing replication
and caching. Designed to support demanding AI workloads, from model training and
inference to autonomous agents, Tigris gives developers the flexibility to build
and scale on their own terms. For more information, visit
[www.tigrisdata.com](https://www.tigrisdata.com).
